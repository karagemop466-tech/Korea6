How "free" is computed

  • Season windows for 2026–2027 are anchored to official released schedules (links on every data page). 2028–2029 are structural estimates and always flagged.
  • Estimated game durations (verified averages): MLB nine-inning 2 h 38 m (MLB.com, 2025 season) · NFL 3 h 03 m incl. 12-min halftime (2025, all 272 games) · NCAAF FBS ~3 h 26 m incl. ~20-min halftime.
