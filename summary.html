Executive Summary

Decision

No vacation window in 2027, 2028, or 2029 is currently certified conflict-free. Available data do not establish complete coverage of every MLB game, all NFL broadcasts, Stanford and Cal football, Westwood One's national NFL broadcasts, and current game-specific local AM/FM carriage for Outer Sunset 94122. We therefore cannot truthfully recommend exact dates or claim that no 7-, 14-, or 21-day window exists.

Use the evidence dashboard to inspect the available official schedule data and the Flagged for Review page for the coverage limitations. Recheck official schedules before booking.

Important corrections

  • The earlier description of July 12–14, 2027 as free is withdrawn: MLB lists the All-Star Game on July 13. MLB's 2027 schedule announcement · MLB Stats API, July 13.
  • February 15–18, 2027 is only a candidate interval between two published league milestones; those milestones alone do not prove that all relevant games or radio broadcasts are absent. The dates are not certified as free. NFL 2026–27 key dates · MLB 2027 Spring Training announcement.
  • No 2028 or 2029 vacation dates are listed as verified here. Values not established by official schedule sources remain blank rather than being projected into calendar entries.

What the planner covers—and does not cover

  • The evidence dashboard includes MLB official schedule snapshots for 2026 and 2027; the 2028–2029 MLB responses are empty/unknown, not proof that those seasons have no games. MLB Stats API example.
  • Its NFL snapshot covers 2026 only; it is not a full future-season NFL feed. Curated Westwood One, 49ers, Stanford, Cal, and other radio entries are partial and not equivalent to complete game-by-game local-carriage verification. NFL schedules · Westwood One NFL schedule.
  • All listed schedule records link to their source where available. Unknown dates/times and uncertain broadcast carriage are flagged. A listed network schedule does not establish that a station is receivable in 94122 on a standard AM/FM radio.
  • Potential free-time gaps based on assumed event durations are estimates, not verified broadcast end times. Baseball can run long; football has overtime and broadcast pre/post-game coverage. See methods and sources.
